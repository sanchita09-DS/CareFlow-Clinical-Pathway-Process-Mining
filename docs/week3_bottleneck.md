# Week 3 — Primary Bottleneck Finding

## Primary Bottleneck

The longest average transition identified in the patient journey is:

**X-Ray → Triage: 27.1 minutes**

This transition occurs in the loop-back pathway:

Registration → Triage → Doctor → X-Ray → Triage → Doctor → Discharge

## Finding

The X-Ray → Triage transition has the highest average transition duration among the observed patient activity transitions.

Because this transition occurs specifically in the loop-back pathway, it is a key area for hospital administrators to investigate when looking for opportunities to reduce patient journey time.

## Supporting Metrics

- Normal pathway average duration: **75.07 minutes**
- Loop-back pathway average duration: **153.81 minutes**
- Additional average time for loop-back cases: **78.74 minutes**
- X-Ray → Triage average transition: **27.1 minutes**

## Recommendation

The hospital should investigate the handoff and waiting process between **X-Ray and Triage** first, particularly for patients who require the loop-back pathway.
